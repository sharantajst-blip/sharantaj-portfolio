Amar Priyo Pet Rabbit: Piko

Pet Animal Story (IMG_20261030_141853.jpg)

Eti amar palito khub-i shundor ebong adorer pet rabbit, tar nam Piko! Piko khub chanchole o shundor shvaber ekti animal. Tar sathe shomoy katate amar eto bhalo lage je ta shobdo kore bujhano jabe na. Tar khabar dewa, tar chola-fera ebong tar abdar amar mon-ke shob shomoy anonde voriye rakhe. Piko amar poribarer ekjon sothik sodoshyo hate dariyeche.

Amar Sohopati O Bondhura

School Friends

Amar Sroddheyo Shikkhokbrindo

Teachers & Education Life

Amar bibhinno sroddheyo shikkhok achen jader kach theke ami niyomito onek kichu shikte perechi ebong jibonke sothik pothe porichalona korar provol onuponera peyechi. Bangladesh Scouts O Amar Ovigota

Discipline, Service & Leadership

Ami gorber shathe Bangladesh Scouts-er ekjon sodoshyo hishebe jukto achi ebong amader school-er scout unit-e shofolvabe Patrol Leader er dayitto palon korechi. Amar Sroddheyo Scout Leader

Mohammad Azharul Islam Sir

Amader scout unit-er mul chalkashokti o pranpurush holen amader sroddheyo unit leader Mohammad Azharul Islam sir. Tini bortomane govt. job theke oboshere thakao tar klantihin porishrom o scout andoloner proti valobasha ektu-o kome nai. Obosher-e jawar poreo tini amader moto torun scout-der utshaho o diknirdeshona diye jacchen ebong shob shomoy biddaloyer scout karjokromke egiye nite nirolosh vabe kaj kore jacchen.

Amar Shikkhaprotisthan

Mirzapur Govt. S. K. Pilot Model High School

Amar shikkhaprotisthan Mirzapur Govt. S. K. Pilot Model High School Tangail jelar ekti ottonto oitihashik o sunamdhonno biddapith. Ei school-er bishal sobuj campus, sushringhol poribesh o chomotkar pathan-pathaner bebostha shikkharthider medha o monon bikashe ottonto guruttopurno vumika palon kore asche. Oitihjo o adhunikotar ek opurbo shomonnoye gore otha ei school-e porashona korte pere ebong er gorvito ongsho hote pere ami sotti bhison anondito o gorvito.

Amar Shohor O Dorshoniyo Sthan

Mirzapur O Tangail Porichiti

Ami muloto Tangail jelar oitihashik o shundor jonopod Mirzapur-e bosobas kori. Amader Mirzapur shohor-ti jemoni shundor, temni er ashepashe royeche kheyal korar moto bibhinno dorshoniyo sthan o oitihashik sthapona. Er moddhe Danbir Ranadaprosad Saha-r protishthito vikhyato Kumudini Complex, ebong Moghul amoler drishontonin sthapotter jonno porichito oitihashik Mohera Zamindar Bari onnyotomo, ja dekhte protidin desher bibhinno pranto theke hazar hazar porjoton ashen.
